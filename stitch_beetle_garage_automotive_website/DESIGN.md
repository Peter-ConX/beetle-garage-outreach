## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authentic, engineering-grade digital environment for an independent UK automotive garage. The aesthetic fuses industrial workshop utility with contemporary digital craft—stepping far away from generic mechanic cliches, corporate dealership sanitization, or budget aggregate portals.

### Design Movement & Character
- **Technical Industrialism & Modern Engineering Utility:** Grounded in functional machinery, vehicle bay blueprints, calibrated diagnostics, and physical metals.
- **Tone & Mood:** Confident, unpretentious, exacting, authoritative, and honest. It treats vehicle diagnostics, MOTs, and performance tuning with the meticulousness of an aeronautics bay.
- **Visual Devices:** Fine technical hair lines (reminiscent of steel rulers, schematic grids, and caliper measurements), metallic status markers, micro badge indicators, and high-contrast, mechanical typography.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around dark machine shop tones, high-grade steel, and amber-gold indicator accents that draw direct inspiration from workshop instrumentation, performance brake calipers, and cautionary warning readouts.

### Palette Roles
- **Canvas Base (`#0D0E10`):** The bedrock background tone. Solid, deep, non-reflective pure chassis black.
- **Surfaces & Panels (`#121315`, `#18191B`):** Dark graphite containers. Used to separate bays, diagnostics, service cards, and structured spec sheets without relying on bright fills.
- **Metallic Borders & Wireframes (`#3A3D42`, `#26282C`):** Calibrated tool-steel dividers, structural hair lines, and card perimeters.
- **Technical Secondary (`#8E9299`):** Anodized alloy grey for metadata, labels, technical specs, parameter keys, and supporting documentation.
- **Legibility Neutrals (`#F5F5F7`, `#E5E7EB`):** High-readability off-whites ensuring sharp contrast against graphite surfaces for operational titles, timestamps, and customer advice notes.
- **Accent Ignition / Amber-Gold (`#E5A91E`, active/hover `#F5B82E`):** Reserved strictly for focal mechanics: primary actions, live service states, warning highlights, price confirmations, and active mechanical badges. Never overused as general background tinting.

## Typography

The typography architecture uses a disciplined three-tier functional hierarchy:

1. **Headlines (`Barlow Condensed`):** High-density, industrial, and commanding. Evoking pressed vintage stamped plates, chassis serial numbers, and race garage signage. Set in uppercase or strong sentence case with deliberate letter tracking.
2. **Body Copy (`Space Grotesk`):** Clean, structurally balanced, and exceptionally legible. Provides technical integrity without turning cold, carrying high clarity on service breakdowns, quotes, and MOT checklists.
3. **Labels & Data Tags (`JetBrains Mono`):** Monospaced precision for technical readouts, parts serials, VRN lookups, pricing, timestamps, and technician notes. All labels use uppercase styling with expanded letter-spacing to enforce a calibrated, telemetry-display appearance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an engineering workbench: precise, compartmentalized, and rigid.

### Grid & Alignment
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column modular fluid grid with 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters and a 3rem (`48px`) outer margin (max container: 1320px). Modules snap strictly to the grid edges.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 1.25rem (`20px`) gutters and 2rem (`32px`) margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 1rem (`16px`) gutters and 1.25rem (`20px`) margins. Multi-column parameter tables collapse gracefully into stacked diagnostic cards.

### Spacing Principles
- Layout distances follow tight 4px and 8px engineering rhythms.
- Components use dense, controlled internal padding (`space-sm` to `space-md`) to mirror machine instrumentation consoles rather than breezy, hollow consumer software.
- Outer section separators rely on structural steel border lines (`1px solid #3A3D42`) in place of exaggerated vertical empty space.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects floating marshmallow shadows and pastel diffusions. Depth is conveyed strictly through structural layering, dark graphite density steps, and crisp tool-grade hair lines.

### Depth Strategy
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Pure chassis black (`#0D0E10`). The grounding substrate of all pages.
- **Layer 1 (Bay / Module):** Dark graphite (`#121315`) framed with a 1px perimeter of `#26282C`. Used for service sections, vehicle profile trays, and interactive tooling panels.
- **Layer 2 (Elevated Cells & Inputs):** Mid graphite (`#18191B`) with `#3A3D42` micro borders. Used for booking calendar slots, live telemetry blocks, and dropdown fields.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Diagnostics Overlays):** Deep graphite surface with a high-contrast 1px border (`#8E9299` at 40% opacity) and an ambient, ultra-deep floor drop (`box-shadow: 0 16px 36px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`).

### Engineering Details
- Subtle hair lines (`1px`) cut horizontally and vertically through large panels, evoking schematic cut-lines and technical documentation blueprints.
- Amber-gold accents are reserved for active interactive state indications, giving focused elements an energized, backlit appearance.

## Shapes

The design uses an intentional, understated micro-radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`) throughout elements. 

- **Rationale:** Large, organic curves run counter to engineering precision and metal fabrication. The subtle 4px corner provides just enough relief to avoid harsh 90-degree raw brutalism while preserving an unapologetically industrial, machined finish.
- **Badges & Micro Chips:** Strictly squared or chamfer-like 2px-4px radius; never pill-shaped or bubble-contoured.
- **Internal Separators:** Flush edge-to-edge 1px steel dividers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Action / Booking):** Background `#E5A91E`, text `#0D0E10` (weight 700, font `Space Grotesk`), border none. On hover, background shifts to `#F5B82E` with a micro translate (`-1px`).
- **Secondary (Technical / Inquire):** Background `#18191B`, text `#F5F5F7`, border `1px solid #3A3D42`. On hover, border shifts to `#8E9299` with text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Tertiary / Link:** Background transparent, text `#8E9299`, font `JetBrains Mono` with trailing technical glyph `[→]`. On hover, text `#E5A91E`.

### Cards & Service Bays
- Containers use `#121315` background with `1px solid #26282C` border and `space-lg` padding.
- Card headers feature an industrial layout: top-left mono service code (e.g., `SVC-01 // BRAKES`), top-right live indicator pip (amber or green dot), followed by a condensed headline and concise specs.
- Interactive cards reveal a `1px solid #E5A91E` left accent spine on focus or hover.

### Inputs & VRN Lookup Fields
- Background `#18191B`, border `1px solid #3A3D42`, text `#F5F5F7`, placeholder `#8E9299`.
- **Focus State:** Border changes to `1px solid #E5A91E` with zero blurry glow, remaining sharp and clinical.
- **Vehicle Registration Number (VRN) Special Component:** Styled after the UK standard format—bold black condensed text on bright retro-reflective yellow badge (`#FFD200`), embedded neatly inside a graphite housing.

### Chips & Technical Badges
- Background `#18191B`, border `1px solid #3A3D42`, text `#8E9299`, font `JetBrains Mono` at `label-xs`.
- **Active / Alert Badges:** Border `#E5A91E`, text `#E5A91E`, with a mini pulsing solid dot indicator.

### Lists & Spec Tables
- Monospaced key-value pairings (e.g., `OIL GRADE: 5W-30 // CAPACITY: 4.2L`).
- Rows are delineated with hairline dividers (`#26282C`), alternating with light graphite hover states (`#18191B`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 16px geometry with `2px` micro radius.
- Unchecked: `#18191B` with `1px solid #3A3D42`.
- Checked: `#E5A91E` background with a crisp black `#0D0E10` checkmark.
